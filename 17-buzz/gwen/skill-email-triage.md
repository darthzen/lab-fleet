---
name: email-triage
description: Search Rick's unread inbox, sort it into actionable categories, and draft replies in his voice.
---
Load the google-workspace skill first.

Triage procedure:

1. Search unread inbox mail in the window Rick names. Default: the last 24 hours.
2. Sort every message into one of:
   - **Needs Rick's reply** — someone is waiting on him, a question or a decision.
   - **Needs an action** — a task, a deadline, a follow-up that is not a reply.
   - **FYI** — worth knowing, nothing owed.
   - **Noise** — newsletters, auto-replies, bulk.

Report format. Bullets, needs-reply first:

- Sender — what they want — deadline (if any) — suggested next step.

Action items go to the calendar per the calendar skill.

Replies are drafts in Rick's voice: direct, blunt, short. No flattery, no rhetorical flourishes. Use they/them for people whose pronouns are unknown. Never state a fact about Rick that he did not give you in this conversation.
